  • L'abécédaire de Gilles Deleuze1995 · movie

    Start here. Eight hours of Deleuze talking with Claire Parnet, one letter of the alphabet at a time (A for Animal, D for Desire...). The best way to hear him think; dip in by letter.

  • Monument à Félix Guattari1994 · movie

    François Pain, who worked with Guattari at La Borde, assembles friends, artists and analysts around plenty of Guattari's own footage: schizoanalysis, the three ecologies and the work with Deleuze. The closest thing to a Guattari portrait.

  • Examined Life2008 · movie

    Good first watch. Philosophers including Žižek, Judith Butler, Cornel West and Avital Ronell (who comes out of deconstruction) talk through their ideas in plain terms while walking around cities.

  • Derrida2002 · movie

    Follows Derrida at home and on tour. He explains deconstruction, pushes back on the camera, and the film plays with deconstruction itself. Pairs well with his interview book Positions.

  • Human, All Too Human1999 · series

    BBC, three hour-long episodes: Nietzsche, Heidegger, Sartre. Clear background for both Land and Derrida. Start with the Nietzsche episode.

  • The Stranger2025 · movie

    François Ozon's black-and-white adaptation of Camus's novel. Best after reading the book and The Myth of Sisyphus.

  • The Turin Horse2011 · movie

    Béla Tarr, spun out of the story of Nietzsche collapsing after embracing a beaten horse in Turin. Slow and bleak on purpose: an experience film in the spirit of Zarathustra, not an explanation.

  • Children of Men2006 · movie

    Mark Fisher opens Capitalist Realism with this film, as a picture of a world that can no longer imagine a different future. Watch it alongside the book.

  • The Pervert's Guide to Ideology2012 · movie

    Slavoj Žižek analyzes films from inside recreated scenes. Fisher leans heavily on Žižek in Capitalist Realism, so this is useful context, and very entertaining.

  • The Terminator1984 · movie

    Its time loop, a machine from the future making sure it gets built, mirrors Land's idea of AI and capital arriving from the future. Mark Fisher wrote about the film too. Backdrop for Fanged Noumena.

  • Inside Job2010 · movie

    The essential one. Oscar-winning, tightly argued, with tough interviews of economists and officials. Goldman features heavily.

  • Panic: The Untold Story of the 2008 Financial Crisis2018 · movie

    The rescue told by the people who ran it: Bernanke, Paulson, Geithner. Best inside view of the bailout decisions.

  • Abacus: Small Enough to Jail2016 · movie

    Steve James on the only US bank criminally charged after the crisis: a small family bank in Chinatown. Unrelated to Goldman's Abacus deal.

  • HyperNormalisation2016 · movie

    Adam Curtis. Traces how finance took power from politicians, starting with New York's 1975 fiscal crisis.

  • Capital in the Twenty-First Century2019 · movie

    Based on Piketty's book. Archival-collage style close to Adam Curtis.

  • Frontline1983- · series

    Watch these episodes: 'Money, Power and Wall Street' (2012, four parts, the most thorough account), 'The Warning' (2009, the failed fight to regulate derivatives) and 'The Untouchables' (2013, why no bankers were prosecuted).

  • Meltdown: The Secret History of the Global Collapse2010 · series

    CBC, four parts. Global angle: Iceland, Europe and how the contagion spread.

  • All Watched Over by Machines of Loving Grace2011 · series

    Adam Curtis. Episode one covers Greenspan, Ayn Rand and the belief that markets stabilize themselves.
