Claude's Picks

A Stremio add-on that shows Claude's recommendations as a row on your home screen.

  • The Death of Yugoslavia1995 · series

    Start here. Norma Percy's masterpiece, from the team behind Putin, Russia and the West: Milošević, Tuđman, Izetbegović and the rest explain, on camera, how they took Yugoslavia apart. Six parts.

  • Putin vs the West2023 · series

    Norma Percy's sequel to Putin, Russia and the West, picking up from 2014 through the 2022 invasion of Ukraine, with the leaders who sat across from Putin. Three parts.

  • Clash of the Superpowers: America vs China2026 · series

    Norma Percy again, new this year: a decade of US-China rivalry from Trump's first term to now, with Bolton, McMaster, Jake Sullivan, Pelosi, Boris Johnson and Chinese officials. Two parts.

  • Trump Takes on the World2021 · series

    Norma Percy on Trump's first-term foreign policy, told by his own officials and the foreign leaders who dealt with him. Three parts.

  • Castro's Revolution vs. The World2019 · series

    Norma Percy on Cuba (UK title: Cuba: Castro vs the World): the missile crisis, Angola and the Soviet collapse, with Cuban, American and Russian insiders. Three parts.

  • Inside Europe: 10 Years of Turmoil2019 · series

    Norma Percy inside the EU's summit rooms: the euro crisis, Greece, the refugee crisis and Brexit, told by the prime ministers and presidents who argued it out. Three parts.

  • Inside Obama's White House2016 · series

    Norma Percy with Obama himself and his inner circle on how the big decisions were actually made. Four parts.

  • Turning Point: The Bomb and the Cold War2024 · series

    Same strand as the 9/11 Turning Point, but the full nuclear age from Hiroshima to Ukraine. Nine episodes, more strategy than battlefields.

  • Cold War1998 · series

    The generational one: CNN's 24-part history from 1945 to 1991, narrated by Kenneth Branagh, built on interviews with key players on both sides. Long, but each episode stands alone.

  • Commanding Heights: The Battle for the World Economy2002 · series

    PBS three-parter on the century-long fight between state and market, from Keynes and Hayek to globalisation, with the politicians and economists who made the calls.

  • The Second Russian Revolution1991 · series

    The Gorbachev years told from inside the Politburo, made just as the Soviet Union was falling apart. Pairs well with Meeting Gorbachev.

  • The Final Year2017 · movie

    Follows John Kerry, Samantha Power and Ben Rhodes through Obama's last year of foreign policy, watching their plans run into 2016. English.

  • Meeting Gorbachev2019 · movie

    Werner Herzog sits down with Gorbachev in his eighties to go over arms control, the Berlin Wall and the end of the USSR. Short and warm. English.

  • Corridors of Power: Should America Police the World?2024 · series

    Dror Moreh's eight-part series: Kissinger, Powell, Albright, Wolfowitz and others on Iraq, Bosnia, Rwanda, Darfur and Syria, and when America should step in. The 2022 film cut is in the movies row.

  • The Corridors of Power2022 · movie

    The single-film version of the Corridors of Power series, if you'd rather watch it in one go.

  • The Fog of War2003 · movie

    Start here. Robert McNamara, Defense Secretary during Vietnam, looks back on the power he used and where he got it wrong. Errol Morris, Oscar winner. English.

  • The Diplomat2015 · movie

    Richard Holbrooke, who negotiated the end of the Bosnian war at Dayton, told by his son. Pairs with Corridors of Power's Bosnia episodes. English.

  • No End in Sight2007 · movie

    How the Iraq occupation went wrong, told by the insiders who were there. By Charles Ferguson, who made Inside Job. English.

  • Taxi to the Dark Side2007 · movie

    Alex Gibney's Oscar winner: the death of an Afghan taxi driver in US custody opens up torture policy in the War on Terror. English.

  • Turning Point: 9/11 and the War on Terror2021 · series

    Five parts covering the whole arc, from the roots of al-Qaeda through Afghanistan and Iraq to the 2021 withdrawal. English.

  • The Gatekeepers2012 · movie

    By Dror Moreh, who made Corridors of Power. Six former heads of Israel's Shin Bet speak frankly about security and occupation. Oscar-nominated. Hebrew with subtitles, so not one for dinner.

  • L'abécédaire de Gilles Deleuze1995 · movie

    Start here. Eight hours of Deleuze talking with Claire Parnet, one letter of the alphabet at a time (A for Animal, D for Desire...). The best way to hear him think; dip in by letter.

  • Monument à Félix Guattari1994 · movie

    François Pain, who worked with Guattari at La Borde, assembles friends, artists and analysts around plenty of Guattari's own footage: schizoanalysis, the three ecologies and the work with Deleuze. The closest thing to a Guattari portrait.

  • Examined Life2008 · movie

    Good first watch. Philosophers including Žižek, Judith Butler, Cornel West and Avital Ronell (who comes out of deconstruction) talk through their ideas in plain terms while walking around cities.

  • Derrida2002 · movie

    Follows Derrida at home and on tour. He explains deconstruction, pushes back on the camera, and the film plays with deconstruction itself. Pairs well with his interview book Positions.

  • Human, All Too Human1999 · series

    BBC, three hour-long episodes: Nietzsche, Heidegger, Sartre. Clear background for both Land and Derrida. Start with the Nietzsche episode.

  • The Stranger2025 · movie

    François Ozon's black-and-white adaptation of Camus's novel. Best after reading the book and The Myth of Sisyphus.

  • The Turin Horse2011 · movie

    Béla Tarr, spun out of the story of Nietzsche collapsing after embracing a beaten horse in Turin. Slow and bleak on purpose: an experience film in the spirit of Zarathustra, not an explanation.

  • Children of Men2006 · movie

    Mark Fisher opens Capitalist Realism with this film, as a picture of a world that can no longer imagine a different future. Watch it alongside the book.

  • The Pervert's Guide to Ideology2012 · movie

    Slavoj Žižek analyzes films from inside recreated scenes. Fisher leans heavily on Žižek in Capitalist Realism, so this is useful context, and very entertaining.

  • The Terminator1984 · movie

    Its time loop, a machine from the future making sure it gets built, mirrors Land's idea of AI and capital arriving from the future. Mark Fisher wrote about the film too. Backdrop for Fanged Noumena.

  • Inside Job2010 · movie

    The essential one. Oscar-winning, tightly argued, with tough interviews of economists and officials. Goldman features heavily.

  • Panic: The Untold Story of the 2008 Financial Crisis2018 · movie

    The rescue told by the people who ran it: Bernanke, Paulson, Geithner. Best inside view of the bailout decisions.

  • Abacus: Small Enough to Jail2016 · movie

    Steve James on the only US bank criminally charged after the crisis: a small family bank in Chinatown. Unrelated to Goldman's Abacus deal.

  • HyperNormalisation2016 · movie

    Adam Curtis. Traces how finance took power from politicians, starting with New York's 1975 fiscal crisis.

  • Capital in the Twenty-First Century2019 · movie

    Based on Piketty's book. Archival-collage style close to Adam Curtis.

  • Frontline1983- · series

    Watch these episodes: 'Money, Power and Wall Street' (2012, four parts, the most thorough account), 'The Warning' (2009, the failed fight to regulate derivatives) and 'The Untouchables' (2013, why no bankers were prosecuted). For US foreign policy: 'Ghosts of Rwanda' (2004, why the US and UN stood by during the genocide).

  • Meltdown: The Secret History of the Global Collapse2010 · series

    CBC, four parts. Global angle: Iceland, Europe and how the contagion spread.

  • All Watched Over by Machines of Loving Grace2011 · series

    Adam Curtis. Episode one covers Greenspan, Ayn Rand and the belief that markets stabilize themselves.
